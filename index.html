Your studies, planned and understood by AI.

OptiStudy is an AI-powered academic workspace that plans your study, answers questions about your own notes and keeps track of what you have actually covered. It is now open to everyone — no waitlist, no invite.

Open OptiStudy at optistudy.in — free to start. Create your account or sign in inside the app.

OptiStudy

  • AI study planner — builds a daily structure from deadlines, workload and free time.
  • Smart schedule and calendar — keeps classes, work and study blocks aligned.
  • Progress tracking — shows what is done, what is next and how much momentum is left.
  • AI chat assistant — answers questions about your own notes.
  • Notes and summaries — turns long sessions into compact study material.

Install OptiStudy on any device

OptiStudy is a progressive web app. It installs straight from your browser on iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS — no app store needed.
